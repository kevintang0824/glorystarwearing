Record the sample ID, size, color, material reference and specification version. Agree the method and acceptance criteria before testing; the checklist below is not a laboratory certification.

Prepare a leggings quotation brief

Copy these fields into your inquiry. Mark unknowns as “please recommend” so a quotation does not depend on unstated assumptions.

  • Brand and destination market; intended activity and wearer.
  • Selected style URL, reference sample or tech pack; seamless or cut-and-sew direction.
  • Size chart; waistband height, rise, inseam, gusset and pocket requirements.
  • Fabric composition if known; hand feel, stretch and opacity requirements.
  • Colors and quantities by style, color and size; expected repeat-order needs.
  • Logo artwork, placement, labels and packaging; sample review criteria.
  • Delivery destination and requested date; identify any fixed launch deadline.

Ask the quotation to separate garment pricing, development or pattern fees, sample charges, branding setup, packaging and freight. Confirm currency, included scope, revision allowances and quote validity in writing.

Private label leggings: buyer questions

What is the MOQ for private label leggings?

For existing leggings styles, the MOQ is 5 pieces per style, and colors and sizes can be mixed. Confirm the available variations for your chosen style. Custom designs, fabrics and branding requirements need a separate quotation; the 5-piece minimum is not a blanket MOQ for custom development.

Can I start with a small leggings order?

Yes. Existing leggings styles can be ordered from 5 pieces per style with mixed colors and sizes. Send the style reference and your quantity split so availability can be confirmed. If you need changes to the design, material or branding, include them in the request for a separate review.

How are custom leggings priced?

Pricing depends on the agreed construction, material, quantity split, branding, packaging and delivery scope. Request separate figures for garments, development, samples and freight so quotations can be compared on the same basis.

How long do sampling and production take?

The sampling lead time is 7 working days. Confirm the sample specification and planned start date with Glory Star Wear. Agree any revision schedule, bulk-production timing and shipping schedule separately; 7 working days describes sampling, not delivery of a bulk order.

What is the difference between seamless and cut-and-sew leggings?

Seamless leggings use a knitting-led construction route; cut-and-sew leggings are assembled from cut fabric panels. Choose according to the required fit, panel lines, texture, pockets and development scope, then verify the details on a sample.

Can I call the leggings squat-proof?

Treat squat-proof as a claim that needs defined evidence. Agree the size, color, lighting, stretch and movement used to assess coverage. A product photo or fabric name alone does not verify opacity, and one sample does not establish results for every variation.

Can I add my logo, pockets and private labels?

Submit the artwork, dimensions, placement, pocket design and label/packaging requirements with the reference style. Feasibility, method and any additional development or setup fees must be confirmed before sampling.

What should I approve before ordering in bulk?

Approve an identified physical sample and the matching specification version, including measurements, fabric reference, colors, construction, artwork, labels and packaging. Record accepted deviations, quality checkpoints and who may authorize production.
