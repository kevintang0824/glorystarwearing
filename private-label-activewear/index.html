Project questions

Private-label activewear FAQ

What is private-label activewear?

Private-label activewear is clothing developed or selected for sale under your brand. The agreed scope can include garment selection or development, logo application, labels, hangtags and packaging.

Can I start without a complete tech pack?

You can start a discussion with a product SKU, reference images or a sketch. Mark the details you have decided and the areas where you need help. Construction, measurements, materials and approval criteria still need confirmation before production.

What determines the MOQ for a private-label project?

MOQ depends on the style, material, color allocation and customization requirements. Ask for quantities by style and color, size-allocation rules and any separate minimums for labels or packaging in the written quotation.

What should I send for a private-label activewear quote?

Send a style reference, estimated quantity by style and color, size range, branding requirements, destination and target timing. Include a tech pack or artwork if available, and identify any details that remain undecided.
