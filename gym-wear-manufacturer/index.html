What to send for a useful quotation

Use these fields as your project checklist. Include known requirements and open questions so the quotation can separate product costs, development, sampling, branding, packaging and delivery.

  • Brand, customer and intended activity for each garment.
  • Style references or tech packs and the role of each product in the collection.
  • Measurement charts, size ranges and fit or coverage requirements.
  • Fabric reference per style, desired feel and measurable performance requirements.
  • Shared color standards, artwork versions, logo placement, labels and packaging.
  • Quantities per style, color and size; destination and requested dates.
  • Sample review criteria and the order in which styles need approval.

Custom Gym Wear for Your Brand: buyer questions

What can a private label gym wear collection include?

A collection can include training tops, shorts, leggings, sports bras, jackets and coordinated sets. Start with the role of each garment and the intended wearer, then define the fit, material and construction separately for each style.

Should I start from catalog styles or develop custom gym clothing?

A catalog reference can provide a starting point when its shape and construction match the brief. A new pattern, material or construction requires a separate development review. Confirm which changes are available on a selected style before comparing price, MOQ and timing.

Which fabric should I choose for gym wear?

Use the garment role, activity, fit and care requirements to shortlist material references. The linked catalog examples show different compositions for tops, shorts and jackets. Composition is a starting point; compare the intended color and construction on a sample before making performance claims.

Is there one MOQ for a mixed gym wear collection?

Do not treat the combined quantity across tops, shorts and other products as a single minimum. Request the MOQ per style and applicable color or customization route, then provide the size split. Confirm any permitted mixing in the quotation.

What affects the price of custom gym clothing?

Price depends on the agreed garment construction, material, quantity split, branding, packaging and delivery scope. Ask for garment prices, development charges, sample charges and freight separately so supplier quotations can be compared fairly.

How should I check a gym wear sample?

Compare the sample with the measurement chart and construction brief, then review it during the intended movement. Record the sample ID, size, color, material, observations and required corrections. Approve the resulting specification version before bulk production.

What should a gym wear inquiry include?

Send the selected style links, product roles, intended activity, size charts, fabric directions, colors, branding and quantities per variation. Include destination and requested timing, and list open questions rather than leaving the supplier to infer them.
