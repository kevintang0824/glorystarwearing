Starting a project

Brief, route, MOQ and sampling

What information should I send with my first inquiry?

Share your company and market, product category, selected style or design route, colors, sizes, estimated quantities, branding, destination and target timing. Mark undecided items clearly. See the custom activewear process.

What is the MOQ for custom activewear?

MOQ is confirmed from the product route, fabric availability, colors, sizes, branding, packaging and quantity split. A project-specific answer requires the complete scope rather than one unsupported number. See the MOQ and sampling guide.

Can I request a sample before bulk production?

A sample route can be discussed before bulk production. Clarify whether you need to evaluate an existing style, branding, fit and construction, a revision or a fully custom design.

What is the difference between selected styles and a custom design?

Selected styles begin from an existing product direction and confirmed customization scope. A custom design begins from your own brief or tech pack and requires construction and specification review.

Controlling the product

Fabric, branding and quality

What logo files and branding details should I provide?

Provide the requested high-resolution PNG or AI artwork, version, colors, dimensions and measured placement. Also define labels, care information, packaging and SKU identification. Use the branding guide.

How should I choose an activewear fabric?

Define the intended activity, wearer, garment type, stretch, recovery, coverage, weight, hand feel and care expectations, then confirm the material on the actual sample. Use the fabric guide.

What should be checked before bulk production and shipment?

Approve the latest specification and identified sample, then agree material, in-process and final inspection controls, measurements, branding, packaging, evidence and release authority. Use the quality-control framework.

Which shipping options can be discussed?

Express, air, rail or sea options can be discussed. The appropriate route requires the confirmed destination, shipment scope, timing, handoff responsibilities and current quotation. Use the shipping brief.

Ask about a real product scope.

Include the product, colors, sizes, branding, estimated quantity and destination so the team can identify the next decision.
