Direct answers

Activewear manufacturing questions

What information should I prepare for a manufacturer?

Prepare company and market context, product category, intended use, reference style or tech pack, measurements, fabric direction, colors, sizes, branding, packaging, estimated quantity by variation, destination and target timing. Mark undecided items clearly.

Which decisions should be made before sampling?

Define the style, measurements, fabric direction, construction, colors, size range, logo artwork and placement, labels, packaging and the exact question the sample must answer.

What should be approved before bulk production?

Approve the latest written specification and identified physical sample, including measurements, material reference, construction, colors, branding, labels and packaging, then agree quality-control checkpoints and release authority.
