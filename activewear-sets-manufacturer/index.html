What to send for a useful quotation

Use these fields as your project checklist. Include known requirements and open questions so the quotation can separate product costs, development, sampling, branding, packaging and delivery.

  • Component list and links or tech packs for every garment.
  • Whether items are sold only as sets, separately, or both.
  • Top and bottom measurement charts and requested size pairings.
  • Color standards and material references for each component.
  • Logo dimensions and placement, labels and set/component SKU logic.
  • Number of complete sets and component pieces by color and size pairing.
  • Packaging, destination, required timing and complete-set approval criteria.

Custom Activewear Sets for Your Brand: buyer questions

What can be included in a custom activewear set?

A set can combine a sports bra, tank or other top with leggings or shorts. Identify every component and state whether the pieces are also sold separately. The quotation should show exactly what is included in one set.

Does MOQ refer to complete sets or individual pieces?

Ask the quotation to state the unit explicitly. Five complete two-piece sets contain ten garments, while five individual pieces do not necessarily make five sets. A leggings-only minimum does not establish the minimum for a complete activewear set.

Can customers mix top and bottom sizes?

Mix-and-match sizing can be requested in the project brief, but available pairings must be confirmed for the selected styles. Provide a top/bottom size matrix, quantities and labeling requirements before quotation and sample approval.

Can two different fabrics match the same color?

A shared color name is not enough to approve a match. Identify the intended color reference, compare the actual components together under agreed lighting, and record whether a match or an intentional contrast is acceptable.

How is the price of a workout set quoted?

Request the included garments, quantity unit, material, branding, packaging and delivery scope in writing. Clarify whether the price is per complete set or per piece, and whether development, samples, labels and freight are included or separate.

What should I approve before producing an activewear set?

Approve the identified samples and specification versions for all components, then review the set together for size pairing, color relationship, branding, labels and packaging. Record accepted differences and who may authorize production.

Can I add coordinated logos, labels and packaging?

Provide the artwork and measured placement for each component, together with set-level and component-level labels and packing instructions. Confirm feasibility and any setup or development charges before approving the complete sample.
