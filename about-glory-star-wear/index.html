Glory Star Wear is the customer-facing brand of Xiamen Guangxingheng Import & Export Co., Ltd., based in Xiamen, Fujian, China. The site publishes activewear categories, product references and a defined project workflow for private-label, OEM and ODM discussions.

Why work with us

One team around the details that shape your collection.

Product selection is only the beginning. A usable brief also needs material direction, fit expectations, branding artwork, labels, packaging and a clear approval path.

Our role is to keep those decisions connected from the first inquiry through sampling and production discussion, so your team can review one coherent project instead of separate fragments.
