Five decisions that improve the first quotation

Precise inputs reduce back-and-forth. If a detail is undecided, say so—the goal is to separate fixed requirements from areas where you want recommendations.

Wearer and market

Describe the customer, sales region and intended price position.

Product roles

List the leggings, bras, tops, shorts or sets required.

Fit direction

Share coverage, length, waistband and support preferences.

Brand details

Provide logo artwork, placement and label expectations.

Project scope

State colors, sizes, quantities and target timing.

Product choices to resolve before sampling

These decisions affect how a yoga product looks, feels and functions. They should be written into the brief rather than left to assumptions.

Coverage and support

Define neckline, back shape, strap direction, cup preference and the activity level the bra is intended to support.

Waist and leg shape

Clarify rise, waistband shape, inseam or length, flare or fitted leg, and whether pockets are part of the design.

Fabric and hand feel

Describe the balance you want between softness, stretch, compression, surface texture and coverage; final selection follows project review.

Procurement questions

Yoga wear manufacturing FAQ

What should a yoga wear tech pack include?

A useful tech pack identifies the style, measurements, fabric direction, colors, construction details, logo placement, label requirements and packaging expectations.

Which products can form a yoga wear collection?

Common building blocks include leggings, sports bras, tanks, short-sleeve tops, shorts and coordinated sets.

Can yoga wear be produced with a private label?

Yes. Discuss logo artwork and label requirements for selected styles, or share a custom design brief for OEM or ODM review.

How are MOQ and sampling details confirmed?

MOQ, sample route and timing depend on the selected style, fabric, colors, customization and quantity. Share those details to receive a project-specific answer rather than relying on a generic number.
