A reliable project moves through written decisions: define the product, confirm specifications and artwork, review samples against those requirements, approve commercial details, then select the shipping route.

Five stages from inquiry to shipping

These stages reflect the workflow stated on the existing Glory Star Wear site. The controls below make each handoff easier to audit.

Contact

Share company, market, product category, requirements, quantities and target timing.

Catalog or design

Select styles and record numbers, or provide a custom design and tech pack.

Customize

Confirm fabric direction, colors, sizes, construction, logo artwork, labels and packaging.

Approve and pay

Resolve sample feedback and confirm the commercial scope and payment route.

Ship

Select express, air, rail or sea based on destination, timeline, quantity and budget.

What belongs in the initial project brief

A useful brief separates facts, targets and open questions. It does not need to be perfect, but it must show which decisions are fixed and which require recommendations.

Business context

Company or brand, target customer, sales region, intended use, price position and launch timing.

Product specification

Reference style or tech pack, measurements, fabric direction, colors, sizes, construction details and quantity by variation.

Brand and delivery

Logo files, placement, labels, packaging, destination, preferred shipping route and the person authorized to approve changes.

How to review an activewear sample

Compare the sample with the approved specification, not only with memory or a reference photo. Feedback should be specific enough for another person to reproduce the same decision.

Fit and measurements

Measure the agreed points, review the intended size on the target wearer and document movement-related issues by location.

Material and construction

Check fabric direction, color, coverage, stretch, seams, edges, cups, pockets and other details named in the brief.

Brand and pack-out

Verify logo scale and placement, labels, care information, packaging, SKU identification and any set-specific requirements.

Buyer questions

Custom activewear process FAQ

What should I send before requesting a quote?

Send your company and market, product category, reference style or tech pack, colors, sizes, estimated quantities, customization requirements and target timing. Mark undecided items clearly.

What should be approved before a sample?

Confirm the style, measurements, fabric direction, construction details, color references, logo artwork and placement, labels and packaging expectations before the sample route begins. Review the MOQ and sampling guide to define which sample decision you need.

How should sample feedback be recorded?

Compare the sample with the approved specification and record each issue by location, measurement or construction detail. Use annotated images and revised documents instead of general comments.

Which shipping options are available?

Glory Star Wear states that projects can ship by express, air, rail or sea. The appropriate route depends on the confirmed timeline, destination, quantity and budget. Use the shipping guide to prepare a comparable route brief.
