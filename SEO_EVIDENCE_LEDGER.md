# SEO / AI evidence ledger

This is the publication gate for factory evidence, cases and third-party references. It prevents search-oriented copy from turning an unverified asset or supplier assumption into a factual claim.

## Publishable facts currently present

- Customer-facing brand: Glory Star Wear.
- Operating entity shown on the site: 厦门广兴恒进出口有限公司 / Xiamen Guangxingheng Import & Export Co., Ltd.
- Published location: Xiamen, Fujian, China.
- Published scope: activewear and sportswear product references, private-label, OEM and ODM project discussions.
- Published contact and official social profiles are kept consistent in the Organization markup and company page.
- The site explains a brief → specification → sample → approval workflow. It is a buyer workflow description, not proof of an owned factory, capacity or certification.

## Evidence not yet publishable as a claim

- Owned-factory address, floor area, headcount, machinery list or production capacity.
- Factory audit, ISO, BSCI, WRAP, OEKO-TEX or other certification claims without the certificate, issuer, scope and validity dates.
- Client names, logos, testimonials, order volumes, delivery results or performance outcomes without written permission and a supporting project record.
- The existing collage and label/packaging images are construction or workflow references unless the owner verifies their provenance and current relevance.

## Required evidence packet before adding a claim

1. Dated original factory photos or video with the site/address and permission to publish.
2. Legal or audit document that matches the named entity, scope, issuer and validity period.
3. A case record with the buyer-approved level of anonymity: product category, brief, quantity band, timeline, market, approved deliverables and measurable outcome.
4. A public third-party profile or reference URL that names the same entity and can be independently checked.
5. Written approval for every customer logo, quote, photo and named case study.

## AI / search publication rule

Use concrete facts, source links, dates and scope qualifiers. Label samples as references. Never use an unrelated brand image as a customer reference, and never imply that a workflow illustration proves current factory ownership or capacity.
