Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Glory Star Wear receives through this website and third-party contact channels, why it is used and what choices are available.

Effective: 28 August 2026Applies to glorystarwearing.com

1. Who is responsible

Glory Star Wear is the customer-facing name used by Xiamen Guangxingheng Import & Export Co., Ltd. (厦门广兴恒进出口有限公司), based in Xiamen, Fujian Province, China. Privacy questions may be sent to kevin@glorystarwears.com.

2. Information we receive

  • Inquiry information: name, business email, telephone or WhatsApp number, country, company or brand, website or social profile, product interests, quantities, timing and project requirements that you choose to provide.
  • Product context: selected product name, SKU and page URL when you begin an inquiry from a product page.
  • Technical and analytics information: page visits, device and browser information, approximate location, referrer and interaction events, but only after analytics permission is given.
  • Communications: messages, files and follow-up information exchanged directly by email, WhatsApp or another agreed channel.

3. How information is used

Information is used to respond to requests, assess product and sampling requirements, prepare or discuss quotations, manage business communications, protect the website, improve content and comply with applicable legal obligations. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on your request for pre-contractual steps, consent, legitimate business interests or legal obligations.

4. WhatsApp and other third parties

The website inquiry tool formats a draft locally in your browser. The page does not upload or store those answers before you choose to open WhatsApp. When WhatsApp opens, the draft is handled by WhatsApp/Meta under its own terms and privacy policy. Website hosting, security and consented analytics may also involve service providers such as Vercel and Google. These providers process information under their own agreements and may operate in other countries.

5. Cookies and analytics choices

Google Analytics is not loaded until you select “Accept analytics” in the website notice. If you decline, essential site pages and inquiry links remain available. Your choice is saved on your device so the site can respect it. See the Cookie Notice or use Cookie settings to change the preference.

6. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support hosting, communications, analytics or project delivery; with professional advisers; with manufacturing, quality or logistics partners when necessary for the requested project; or with authorities where legally required. Only information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose should be shared.

7. Retention and security

Business inquiry and project records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for follow-up, project administration, dispute prevention, accounting or legal obligations. Retention varies by the relationship and record type. Reasonable organizational and technical safeguards are used, but no internet or messaging service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent. Email the address above with enough information to identify the relevant communication. You may also complain to the data-protection authority that applies to you.

9. Children and policy updates

This B2B website is not directed to children. We may update this policy when the website, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Submitting a business inquiry does not subscribe you to unrelated marketing. Any future marketing choice should be offered separately.
