What to send for a useful quotation

Use these fields as your project checklist. Include known requirements and open questions so the quotation can separate product costs, development, sampling, branding, packaging and delivery.

  • Intended activity, wearer and market; reference style or tech pack.
  • Measurement chart, size range, underband, neckline and coverage.
  • Strap shape, adjustment, back construction and closure requirements.
  • Cup or pad system, lining, material reference and colorways.
  • Logo artwork, placement dimensions, labels and packaging.
  • Quantity per style, color and size; destination and target date.
  • Sample questions, fit-test method and who approves the final specification.

Custom & Private Label Sports Bras: buyer questions

How do I choose a private label sports bra manufacturer?

Compare suppliers using the same style reference, activity, size chart, cup system, material and branding brief. Request a sample, review the actual fit and construction, and confirm the legal entity, quotation scope and quality checkpoints before approving production.

What is the MOQ for custom sports bras?

Ask for the minimum against your chosen sports bra style, color and customization route, and supply quantities by size. A minimum quoted for leggings or another category does not establish the minimum for sports bras. Confirm it in the written quotation.

Can I choose removable pads, fixed pads or no padding?

Specify the preferred cup system in the brief. Selected catalog references list removable pads; other constructions need a feasibility review for the chosen style. Check the pad pocket, placement and care behavior on the sample before approval.

Does a higher spandex percentage mean a higher-support sports bra?

Fabric composition alone does not verify support. Define the intended wearer and activity along with the band, straps, coverage and cup construction. Evaluate the complete sample against agreed fit and movement criteria.

What should I send for a sports bra quotation?

Send a style URL or tech pack, activity, size chart, fabric direction, band and strap details, cup system, colors, quantities by variation, logo artwork, labels, packaging and destination. Identify decisions that still need a recommendation.

Can I add my logo and private labels?

Logo, label and packaging requirements can be reviewed with the selected style. Provide the artwork version, dimensions and placement, and confirm application method, feasibility, setup costs and sample scope before ordering.

How do sampling and bulk-production schedules differ?

Ask for separate dates for sample preparation, feedback, any revisions, approval, production and shipping. Confirm the specification and start conditions for each stage. A sample schedule should not be presented as the delivery time for a bulk order.
