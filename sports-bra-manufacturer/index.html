Start with use, not decoration

The same visual concept can require different construction when intended for yoga, studio training, running or everyday athleisure. Define the wearer and activity before selecting the neckline, straps and cup system.

Five inputs for a useful sports bra brief

A quote is more reliable when the team can distinguish required features from optional ideas. Provide a tech pack when available, or organize the brief around these decisions.

Activity

State whether the style is for yoga, studio, running, training or lifestyle use.

Fit

Define neckline, side coverage, back shape, underband and desired length.

Support

Describe the wearer and movement instead of relying only on a support label.

Construction

Record strap, cup, lining, closure and seam preferences.

Commercial scope

List colors, sizes, logo placement, quantities and target timing.

Details to verify on the sample

A sample is not only a color check. Review it against the wearer, movement and measurements defined in the brief, then record decisions before bulk manufacturing.

Fit and coverage

Check neckline position, side coverage, underband stability, strap placement and whether the intended size fits as specified.

Movement and support

Evaluate the sample during the intended activity. Record specific issues rather than only marking the style “comfortable” or “supportive.”

Material, construction and branding

Review fabric, cups, lining, seams, edges, logo scale and placement, labels and the overall finish against the approved brief.

Buyer questions

Sports bra manufacturing FAQ

What information is needed to quote a custom sports bra?

Provide the intended activity, reference style or tech pack, size range, fabric direction, coverage, strap and cup preferences, colors, logo placement and estimated quantities.

How should a brand define sports bra support?

Define the intended activity and wearer, then specify coverage, straps, underband, cup construction and fit expectations. Support should be evaluated on the confirmed sample rather than treated as an unsupported label.

Can sports bras use removable or fixed cups?

Cup construction can be discussed as removable, fixed or unpadded, depending on the style and project requirements. Document the selected construction before sampling.

Can I add a private label and logo?

Yes. Share high-resolution logo artwork, placement, label and packaging requirements so the customization route can be reviewed with the selected style.
