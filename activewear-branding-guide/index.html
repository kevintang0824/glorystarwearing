Build a logo specification, not only a mockup

The appropriate application route must be confirmed against the actual style, material, artwork and project scope. This guide does not assume every method is suitable or available for every garment.

Master artwork

Supply the requested high-resolution PNG or AI file. Name the approved version and preserve the original proportions.

Measured placement

Identify garment view, reference point, finished dimensions, orientation, color and acceptable position tolerance.

Material compatibility

Approve appearance on the actual fabric, color and garment construction rather than relying only on a digital mockup.

Five approvals in a private-label brand system

Treat branding as connected product data. A change to artwork, garment color, size label or pack-out should update the controlled specification.

Logo artwork

File, version, colors, dimensions and placement.

Brand label

Content, material direction, dimensions and position.

Size and care

Size logic, care and fiber information, language and location.

Packaging

Folding, bag, insert, barcode and SKU identification needs.

Approval record

Accepted sample, dated specification and authorized approver.

Review branding on the physical sample

Artwork approval on screen is not enough. Check the finished garment and pack-out against the latest specification.

Garment appearance

Check scale, orientation, placement, color relationship, edges and how the result changes when the garment stretches.

Label information

Verify content, size, position, legibility and consistency with the intended product and destination requirements.

Pack-out accuracy

Confirm the correct product, color, size, labels, packaging and SKU identification remain associated.

Buyer questions

Activewear branding FAQ

What logo file should I send?

Glory Star Wear asks buyers to provide a high-resolution PNG or AI logo file. Keep the master artwork, color references and approved version with the project specification.

What should a logo placement specification include?

Identify the garment and view, placement reference, finished width and height, color, orientation and acceptable position tolerance. Use an annotated drawing instead of “left side.”

What label information should a brand prepare?

Identify label types, brand and size presentation, care and fiber information, destination-market requirements, language, position, dimensions and artwork version. Confirm legal requirements with a qualified adviser.

How should branding be approved before bulk production?

Review logo scale, placement, color and appearance on the actual material, plus label content, packaging and SKU identification, against the latest approved specification and sample.

Prepare one source of truth for your brand details.

Send the artwork, color references, placement dimensions, label content, packaging needs and estimated quantities.
