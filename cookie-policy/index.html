Cookie Notice

The core website works without optional analytics. This notice explains the small amount of device storage used to remember your choice and what happens if analytics is accepted.

Effective: 28 August 2026Analytics is opt-in

1. What cookies and similar storage are

Cookies and browser storage let a website remember information on a device. This website uses local browser storage to remember whether you accepted or declined optional analytics. The preference does not contain your inquiry answers.

2. Essential preference storage

The key gsw_analytics_consent records “accepted” or “declined” on your device. It is necessary to avoid asking on every page. Removing browser site data clears this preference and causes the notice to appear again.

Language preference

The key gsw_locale records the website language you select so the homepage can reopen that saved-language version. It does not contain inquiry answers and it does not send page content to a translation service.

3. Optional Google Analytics

If you accept, the site loads Google Analytics measurement ID GT-W6K5676W to understand pages visited and basic interaction events. Advertising storage and personalized advertising signals are disabled in the site configuration. Google may still process technical information under its own policies. If you decline, the Google Analytics script is not loaded by this consent control.

4. WhatsApp and external links

WhatsApp, social networks and other external sites are not loaded merely because you view a Glory Star Wear page. When you follow an external link, that provider may use its own cookies or storage under its policies.

5. Change your choice

Select Cookie settings to reopen the choice panel. You may also clear site data through your browser. Declining analytics does not block product pages, guides, email or WhatsApp contact links.

6. More information

See the Privacy Policy for information handling and rights, or email kevin@glorystarwears.com with a question.
