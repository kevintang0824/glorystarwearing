MOQ, sample scope and quotation checklist

Activewear MOQ & Sampling Guide

An activewear MOQ is the minimum quantity accepted for a defined order scope. Ask whether it applies per style, color, fabric or branding method, and confirm the size split. Sample fees, revision scope and timing should be quoted separately against the same brief.

Why one generic MOQ can mislead a buyer

The same total units can represent very different production scopes. Ten variations with small quantities are not equivalent to one style and one color at the same total volume.

Style and construction

A selected style and a new custom construction require different preparation and validation. Define which route you need.

Fabric and color

Availability, special material requirements and the number of colors can change how a project is evaluated.

Sizes and branding

Show quantity by size and color, plus logo method, labels and packaging—not only a collection total.

Choose the sample question you need answered

“I need a sample” can mean several different things. Name the decision the sample must support.

Style evaluation

Review an existing style, material direction and general fit.

Branding evaluation

Check logo scale, placement, label and packaging direction.

Fit sample

Compare measurements, construction and movement with the brief.

Revision sample

Verify documented corrections against the latest version.

Approval reference

Identify the accepted version used with the final specification.

Send a brief that can receive a specific answer

List unknown items instead of guessing. A structured brief makes it possible to identify what still needs confirmation before MOQ, sample scope, price or timing can be discussed.

Product

Category, intended use, reference style or tech pack, measurements, construction and fabric direction.

Variations

Colors, sizes and estimated quantity for each style-color-size combination.

Commercial context

Branding, labels, packaging, destination, target timing and which decisions remain open.

Confirm the sample scope before paying

A sample quotation should make the deliverable and the next decision clear. Ask the supplier to complete the unanswered items below.

  • Sample purpose: style evaluation, fit, branding, revision or approval for production.
  • Included work: pattern, material, colors, size, logo, labels and packaging actually represented.
  • Charges: development, sample, revisions and delivery listed separately; any refund or credit conditions in writing.
  • Timing: when work starts, which approvals or materials are required, and the estimate for dispatch and transit.
  • Feedback: who reviews the sample, how corrections are recorded, and how another revision is priced.
  • Approval record: sample version, specification, remaining differences and the checks required before bulk release.

Buyer questions

Activewear MOQ and sampling FAQ

What is the MOQ for custom activewear?

MOQ is the minimum quantity accepted for a defined order scope. Confirm whether it applies per style, color, fabric or branding method, and confirm the size split. Glory Star Wear quotes the applicable minimum after reviewing the selected style or custom design, materials and customization requirements.

What should an activewear sample quotation include?

It should identify the sample purpose and version, materials and customization included, development and sample charges, revision terms, delivery cost, estimated timing and the approval needed before bulk production. Any sample-fee credit should be stated in writing.

Can I order a sample before bulk production?

A sample route can be discussed before bulk production. Clarify whether you need to evaluate an existing style, branding application, fit and construction, or a fully custom design.

Does sample approval guarantee every bulk garment?

No. The approved sample and specification create the reference for production, but the bulk order still needs agreed quality-control checkpoints and release criteria.

What information is needed to confirm MOQ and sampling?

Provide product category, reference style or tech pack, fabric direction, colors, sizes, branding, packaging, estimated quantity by variation, destination and target timing.
