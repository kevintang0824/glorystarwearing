Build Your Activewear Brand with a Clear Project Brief

A practical path from catalog selection to bulk production

1. Contact: Send your company and product requirements through WhatsApp so our team can respond directly.
2. Catalog: Choose the styles you need and record the style number, color, size and quantity.
3. Customize: Send your logo in a high-resolution PNG or AI file so we can prepare the artwork.
4. Payment: After all details are confirmed, arrange payment by credit card, PayPal or T/T.
5. Shipping: We ship by express, air, rail or sea according to your timeline and budget.
